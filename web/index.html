Fills your own travel log workbook from your bookings, in exactly its format: every ticked row stays as it is, unticked rows get the country, arrival and departure times from your flights and hotels, and new countries are added to the map list. Settings

Bookings by email

Forward booking confirmations to your travel address. The NAS reads the email and its attachments; the bookings are added the next time the app is open.

Work calendar invites

Business trips and their flights are sent to your work email as Outlook invites and kept up to date. Private trips only when you choose Send in the trip. Trips that have ended are not sent.

…

Reading

Server-side document reading

With AI reading on, the original file is sent to the Anthropic API (Claude) for reading; every date, time and time zone it returns is re-checked on the NAS. With AI off, the local readers are used. No bookings or payments are made.

Data and backup

Local workspace

Backups include your documents and are not encrypted: store them privately. Excel contains records, not the documents. The server keeps earlier revisions; removing a record does not erase history.

Help and limits
  1. Forward a booking email, drop a file or paste text. Claude (if configured) or the NAS reader reads it; every date and time zone is checked; clear matches are added to the right trip, one item per flight leg. The rest waits in the Inbox with a reason.
  2. Watch for Saved on NAS. Open the same address on another device; changes from several devices are merged.
  3. For a PDF open a trip and choose PDF → Open printable itinerary → Print / Save as PDF.
  4. Download backups regularly.
Private is a trip label, not a separate account. Everyone with the application password can see all trips. Claude and OCR can misread: verify travel details before you travel.

Paste a confirmation or email

The text is stored on your NAS, read, matched to a trip and added to the itinerary when the details are clear. Otherwise it appears in the review queue. No booking or payment is made.

Your email key

Copy this key into the Cloudflare Worker as the secret INBOUND_SECRET. It is shown only now; Travel Manager keeps just a fingerprint of it. The previous key, if any, no longer works.

Reading memo

Claude reads this before every document. Write your own preferences at the top. When you correct something Claude read, a short line is added automatically under “Learned from your corrections”, so the same mistake is avoided next time. Edit or delete any line.

Prepare itinerary PDF

A clean itinerary opens in a new window. Choose Print / Save as PDF there, then select Save as PDF. No travel data is sent to a PDF service.
